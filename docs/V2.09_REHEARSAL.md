# Winter Lights Encore

Open Projection Mapper v2.09 build 28. Choose **File → Open Project** and open **Winter Lights Encore.projectionmap** from **assets/demos** in the v2.09 checkout. Use **Save Project As** if you want a fresh copy to edit. The package includes its 24-second instrumental soundtrack, image, and video. The singing ornament is drawn by the app and its mouth shapes are keyed to the music.

## Play the show

In **Show**, choose **Test Window**, then click **Open Output** and **Play**. Use **Fixtures** to see the simulated moving head, plug, and strips while the Test Window shows the projection.

| Time | What to look for |
|---|---|
| 4.75–8 s | Happy Holidays spans the linked panorama over snowfall and golden sparkles. |
| 4–16 s | The Video Window's outline animates independently of the video in the center. Both have a transition at 12 seconds. |
| 12–16 s | The singing ornament appears on the Pattern Tile. Its mouth shapes follow the key diamonds on the timeline. The lantern's diamond-shaped cutout stays visible beside it. |
| 16.75–20.5 s | The panorama counts down to the Finale marker at 20 seconds and holds zero briefly. |

## Edit a copy

- Select the panorama, then open **Layers** in the Edit inspector. Select **Golden Sparkles** to change its blend or opacity. Select **Snowfall Greeting** to edit the wording, color, or placement. Its clip defines when it appears.
- Select **Video Window** and open **Regions → Window Outline**. Adjust the thickness or turn on **Edit region on canvas** to drag points or draw a polygon. Use **Region shape** to save the polygon for another region.
- Select the **Singing Ornament** lane on the timeline. Scrub to a beat, choose a mouth shape in **Layers**, and click **Key mouth at playhead**. Click a diamond to seek to that key or right-click it to replace or delete it. Loop 12–16 seconds to compare your edit.
- Select **Finale Countdown** and choose a different marker or enter a target time. Scrub forward and back to compare the same timestamp.

The timeline's track header can collapse the layer and region lanes. The bottom clip controls still trim and crossfade clips on their own lanes.
