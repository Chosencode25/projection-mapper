# Record lighting cues to a song

1. Open a show with a soundtrack and at least one saved lighting cue or look. Switch to **Lighting** and move the playhead to where you want the take to begin.
2. Click **Record Cues**. Playback starts or continues from the playhead. With a loop enabled, playback starts inside the loop. Use **GO** to trigger the next cue, pick another cue in **Next cue**, or select a saved look and click **Trigger Look**. The counter increments when a trigger is captured.
3. Click **Stop Recording**. Playback pauses and the review opens. Pausing, seeking, reaching the song or loop boundary, stopping fixtures, or leaving Lighting also ends the take. When returning to Lighting, choose **Review Take** if needed.
4. Check the cues you want. You can adjust their song times in seconds, or use **Snap Selected to Beats** if the show has beat markers. **Restore Recorded Times** returns to the original trigger times. If two cues target the same fixture at exactly the same time, adjust one time or uncheck a cue.
5. Click **Add Selected to Timeline**. The approved cues become ordinary lighting cues you can move, edit, or delete on the shared timeline. **Undo Add Recorded Cues** removes the whole insertion. Save the show to keep it.

Recording creates snap cues from the fixture states of each trigger. The original cues keep their timing and transition settings. You can set motion lead and dimmer fade on an added cue in its editor.

**Discard Take** clears the pending recording. **Close** keeps the captured triggers available in the current show for review. Add the take before changing shows or closing the app.
