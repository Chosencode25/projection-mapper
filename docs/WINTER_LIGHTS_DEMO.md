# Winter Lights Playground

Open Projection Mapper v2.08 build 27, then use **File → Open Project** and choose **Winter Lights Playground.projectionmap** in **assets/demos**. The project includes a 24-second original bell-and-bass soundtrack and all its media. Use **Save Project As** when you want to keep a fresh copy of the playground.

## Watch the show

Open **Test Output**, switch to **Show**, then press **Play**. Switch the Show preview between **Projection** and **Fixtures** to inspect either side while the Test Output window is open.

| Time | What to explore |
|---|---|
| 0–4 s | The linked panorama twinkles across three faces. Below it are a masked lantern, looping video and an image tile. |
| 4–5 s | The panorama fades into snowfall while the image tile fades into ornaments. |
| 8–9 s | Snow blends into candy-cane stripes. The simulated roofline starts a bouncing color chase at 8.5 seconds. |
| 12–13 s | Two ranges of the video blend in the video window. The panorama fades into ornaments. |
| 16.5–19.5 s | The projection wave runs across the lantern, video window and tile. |
| 20–24 s | The panorama ends in stars and the moving head and roof strips switch to the finale look. |

## Play with the controls

- **Loop the chorus.** In Edit, turn on **Draw Loop** and drag across the waveform from **8 to 16 seconds**, or enter those times in the Song menu.
- **Inspect a fade.** Jump to the **Crossfade midpoint** marker at **8.5 seconds**. Select the incoming clip and open **Transition** to change the duration or easing.
- **Edit a wave.** Select **Across the Stage** on the Choreography lane. Change the target positions or direction and replay from **16.5 seconds**.
- **Try mapping and masks.** Select **Masked Lantern**, choose **Selection** on the canvas, then edit its boundary or diamond-shaped hole. Use Undo to compare.
- **Try manual lighting.** In Show, select **Manual Encore** from the manual cue picker and press **GO**. Use **Release to Timeline** to return to the authored sequence. From **Beat Queue**, choose a named lighting look for the **Next Bar** and watch the countdown.

The project also includes six saved holiday effect presets, two palettes, named fixture groups, beat and downbeat markers, and simulated fixture placement. The soundtrack owns playback audio. The video windows are muted.
