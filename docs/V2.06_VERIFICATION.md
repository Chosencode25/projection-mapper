# v2.06 — Timeline and Cues Verification

November 9, 2026. Bundle 2.06/build 21. Portable format 12. Developed from tested v2.05 on `codex/v2.06-timeline-cues`.

## Behavior under test

- Timed pan/tilt can snap, interpolate linearly or ease in and out. Dimmer fades independently. Timed transitions reach the cue at its timestamp. Earlier starts are clamped to the preceding cue for that fixture. Relay, color-wheel and gobo values change on the cue.
- GO latches its fixtures until another manual cue replaces their state or the operator releases, seeks, loops or stops. Direct fixture overrides and blackout have priority. Manual motion and dimmer changes use the configured durations even when the song is paused. Manual-only cues are excluded from timed evaluation.
- The marker rows share the existing time scale, waveform and transport. Edits, suggested beats and batch import use the existing undo/recovery path. Marker snapping is available to clip moves/trims, lighting cues and drawn loops.
- Format 12 adds optional markers and transition data. Format 11 soundtracks and existing face/object clip ownership, times, media, mapping and fixture states are retained. Existing validation rejects overlapping clips on the same content owner and keeps gaps dark.
