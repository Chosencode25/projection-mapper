# v2.06 — Timeline and Cues Rehearsal

Open **Timeline and Cues Rehearsal.projectionmap** from the local v2.06 build folder. Open Test Output and choose **Tools → Fixtures & Simulation → 2D Preview**. The song loops from 3 to 7 seconds. Clear the loop from the **Song** menu to play the full track.

- **Timed moves.** Select the Blue Finale diamond. The pan/tilt lead is one second and Move is set to Ease In/Out. The head starts turning at 4 seconds, is halfway at 4.5 and arrives at 5. The dimmer also fades over that second. The RGB strip and plug switch on the 5-second cue. Snap retains the earlier immediate movement behavior.
- **Markers.** Use the flag button to add or edit Beat, Lyric and Section markers. Use the metronome to tap a beat at the current song time. Use the arrow buttons or click a marker to navigate. With Snap enabled, nearby markers take priority over whole seconds for cue and clip placement. Suggest Beats uses waveform transients and the resulting marks can be edited.
- **Import markers.** Choose Markers → Paste Markers and paste one tab-separated row per marker. Fields are seconds, kind and name. For example, use three fields with the values 5, Beat and Blue Finale. A malformed row leaves the current markers intact.
- **Loop from the waveform.** Enable Draw Loop and drag from one point in the waveform to another. With Draw Loop off, click the waveform to move the playhead. Use the Song menu to fine-tune or clear the loop.
- **Fixture groups.** Give fixtures a group in Edit Patch. Choose a look and a group in the Lighting row before placing a cue. The cue stores the look entries for that group's current members.
- **Manual cues.** Open Cue List, pick a cue and press GO. GO takes over the cue's fixtures, advances the list and uses the configured move and fade durations. Mark a cue Manual Only to keep it off automatic playback. Use Release to Timeline to hand control back, or Stop Fixtures to darken the output. Seek, loop wrap and Stop reset the manual list. Direct fixture overrides and blackout retain their priority.

Projection clips continue to address their existing stable face or linked-object owner. A face in a linked object uses the shared owner track, and overlapping clips on that owner are rejected. Gaps remain dark. Different owners can play at the same time. Format 12 preserves previous song, media, geometry and fixture data. Save a separate copy when you still need to open a project in v2.05.
