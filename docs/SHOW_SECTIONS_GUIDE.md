# Reuse a show section

Use the **Timeline** menu in **Edit** while playback is paused.

1. In the first show, choose **Save Show Section…**. Name the section and enter its start and end times. Give the included tracks and fixtures useful role names, such as Roof, Windows, or Roof Strip. Save the .projectionsection package.
2. Open the receiving show, position its playhead, and choose **Insert Show Section…**. Open the saved section. Review the suggested matches and choose a compatible destination for every role. You can enter a different insertion time before continuing.
3. Click **Insert Section**. Projection clips and timed lighting cues are placed relative to the selected time. The receiving map, masks, and fixture patch stay in place. Undo and redo treat the section as one edit.

The package includes the images and videos used by the selected clips. Move the entire package if you want to reuse it on another Mac. The receiving show keeps its own song. Prepare it in Show Mode when you want to rehearse the result.

A matching layer or region must already exist to receive a clip on that track. Its placement, text style and mouth keys come from the receiving show. Manual cues, separate movement paths and choreography tracks remain outside this first section workflow.

A range may trim a clip, including its video source. A trimmed procedural effect starts its phase at the new clip start. Choose a range that includes an entire crossfade or leaves it outside the selection. Conflicting destination clips or same-fixture cue times must be resolved before insertion. Each section role needs a distinct destination.
