# v2.05 — Song rehearsal

Open **Song Sync Rehearsal.projectionmap** from the local v2.05 build folder for a short rehearsal with a generated ten-second click track. The red look starts at zero, the head receives its next pan target at four seconds, and the blue look starts on the accented click at five seconds.

1. Open **Test Output** and optionally switch the editor to **Tools → Fixtures & Simulation → 2D Preview**. The waveform and fixture cues share the ruler.
2. Press **Play**. Pause and click or drag on the ruler to inspect a specific moment. Press **Stop** to rewind and stop the fixtures.
3. In the timeline's **Song** menu, choose **Set Loop Range**, then enter **3** and **7** seconds. Play and watch the blue cue reset as the loop returns to the red look. Clear the loop in the same menu.
4. To use your own file, open a project and choose **Add Song** in the timeline. Save a separate rehearsal copy if you also use v2.02. The newer project format includes the audio. Removing or replacing a song can be undone.
5. Use the song volume control or **Mute Soundtrack** for silent rehearsal. Use **Audio-Only Rehearsal** to listen without projection content and simulated lighting. The song menu also allows selection of a Mac audio output device. Projects with a song use the song for audio and keep embedded video audio silent.

Song playback is 1× in this build. Pan and tilt still use the fixture cues authored in v2.02. The Test Window and simulated fixtures require no external devices. Verify actual listening and appearance on your Mac.
