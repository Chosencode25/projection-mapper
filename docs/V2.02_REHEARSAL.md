# v2.02 lighting rehearsal

1. Open a project with a projection clip and open **Test Mode**. Choose **Tools → Fixtures & Simulation** in the editor. The timeline stays below the fixture workspace.
2. Add one DMX controller and one Arduino controller. Leave both profiles on **Simulation**. Patch a **Moving Head** in CH 9 at DMX address 001, a **Switched Plug** at Arduino output 1, and an **RGB Strip** at Arduino outputs 2, 3, and 4 in red, green, blue order. Try assigning the plug to output 2 and verify the patch is rejected.
3. Click **Resume Fixtures**. Set a head position, dimmer and color, turn on the plug, and choose a strip color. Tick the boxes by the fixture names and capture a look. Change the controls and capture a second look, then clear the manual overrides so they do not take priority over the cues.
4. In the timeline's **Lighting** row, choose the first look and click the plus button to place it at the playhead. Move the playhead and place the second look. Select its diamond and enter a pan/tilt lead. The monitor should show the head position changing ahead of the second cue while dimmer, color, gobo and relay values wait for the cue.
5. Play, pause and scrub in both directions. Check that the monitor and projection follow the same playhead. Try a manual override, fixture blackout, and **Stop Fixtures**. Fixture blackout and fixture Stop affect the simulated lights independently of projection. Timeline Stop returns the playhead to zero and stops the fixture simulation. Resume Fixtures or start playback again to restore the cue state at the playhead.
6. Toggle **Inject Failure** on one controller. Its monitor should show a simulated failure while the other controller and Test Mode keep running. Clear the failure, then repeat on the other controller.
7. Save and reopen a rehearsal copy. Confirm patches, looks and cues remain editable and fixture output opens dark. The v2.02 app saves format 10. Keep a v2.01 copy if you also need to open the project in the earlier app.

DMX reports simulated fixture values and assigned slots. Arduino shows the simulated on/off values for each assigned relay. Loop-wrap behavior is exercised in automated evaluation tests. The song loop controls arrive in v2.05.

## Graphical fixture preview

In build 18, choose **Tools → Fixtures & Simulation → 2D Preview**. Use **Edit Placement** to drag the fixtures into position. For an RGB strip, drag either endpoint to lay out the line. Save the project to keep the placements.

Play or scrub the existing timeline with the preview open. The moving head's cone shows its pan, tilt, color and brightness schematically. A dashed line indicates its aim when dark. A switched plug gets a glow when on, and the RGB strip displays its selected color. Fixture blackout extinguishes these lights while retaining their placement. An unavailable simulated controller is labeled on its fixture markers.

In the saved rehearsal, the head changes aim at four seconds. The strip changes from red to blue at five seconds. This view follows the current cue values, including snap changes. You can keep the separate Test Output window open to watch the mapped projection.
